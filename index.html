QUICK ANSWERS

Before you hand us the dust...

These answers keep expectations clear without making customers dig through fine print.

Do I have to know which cleaning service I need?

No. Choose the closest option on the quote form and describe the space. The service can be matched to the actual cleaning need.

Can I request a one-time cleaning?

Yes. The site is designed to support one-time, move-related, deep-clean, commercial, and recurring requests.

Can I ask for specific areas to receive extra attention?

Absolutely. Use the quote form to note problem areas or priorities so they are part of the conversation from the beginning.

How much will my cleaning cost?

Pricing depends on the space, condition, service type, frequency, and any special requests. That is why the site leads with a free quote rather than a generic price that may not fit your job.
